# Data Acquisition and Management Guide

## Datasets
1. **Primary Discovery Cohort (GSE136831)**
   - Source: NCBI GEO (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE136831)
   - Scope: 286,675 single cells across 78 human donors (IPF, COPD, and Control).
   - Expected location: `data/raw/GSE136831/`

2. **External Validation Cohort (GSE159354)**
   - Source: NCBI GEO (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE159354)
   - Scope: Independent cohort of ~180,000 single cells.
   - Expected location: `data/raw/GSE159354/`

Sample metadata and audit manifests are catalogued in `data/metadata/`.
